# Returns & Exchanges

_Last updated: [DATE]_

We want you to love your FlySox. If something's not right, here's how returns and exchanges work under the Consumer Protection Act (CPA).

## Faulty or Incorrect Items
If your socks arrive damaged, faulty, or not what you ordered, contact us within 7 days of delivery at [returns@flysoxsa.co.za] with your order number and a photo. We'll arrange a replacement or full refund, including return shipping, at no cost to you.

## Change of Mind
For hygiene reasons, socks can only be exchanged or returned if they are unworn, unwashed, and still in their original packaging with tags attached, within 14 days of delivery. Change-of-mind returns are at the customer's cost for return shipping.

## How to Start a Return
1. Email [returns@flysoxsa.co.za] with your order number and reason for return.
2. We'll send you return instructions and an address.
3. Once we receive and inspect the item, we'll process your refund or exchange within 7 business days.

## Refunds
Refunds are made to your original payment method. Delivery fees are non-refundable for change-of-mind returns.

This policy is provided in addition to, and does not limit, your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2005.
