# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

FlySox SA ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

## Information We Collect
- Contact details (name, email, phone number, delivery address) provided at checkout
- Order and payment information (processed securely by our payment partner; we do not store your card details)
- Basic technical data (IP address, browser type, pages visited) via cookies and analytics

## Why We Collect It
- To process and deliver your order
- To communicate with you about your order, returns, or support queries
- To improve our website and product range
- To send marketing communications, only if you've opted in (you can unsubscribe at any time)

## How We Protect It
We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to keep your information secure, and only share it with service providers who need it to fulfil your order (e.g. our courier and payment gateway).

## Your Rights
Under POPIA, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, and to object to how it's processed. To exercise these rights, contact us at [privacy@flysoxsa.co.za].
