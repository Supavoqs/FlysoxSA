## Adding more designs later
Pick a design from the supplier catalog, give it a FlySox-style name (see `docs/CONTENT.md` for tone), add a row here with its supplier design #, then add a matching row to `data/products-import.csv` (see that file's format, including its `Meta: _yoast_wpseo_metadesc` column) and re-import.
